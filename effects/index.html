3D Effects

Add-on visual effects for 3D-TVision. Three are free - download them below. More are on the way.

Free effects

Download one at a time, or all three at once: test_effects_pack.zip (pack version 1.0.0)

The same pack comes with 3D-TVision (in modules\ex_mode), so you may already have it. Needs 3D-TVision 1.0.1 or newer. When an effect gets a new version, download it again and add it: the app asks, then replaces the old one.

Julia Drift Free

Installing

  1. Download a .zip. There is no need to unpack it.
  2. In 3D-TVision, open the MODE list.
  3. Under the white line, press the folder button and pick the .zip.
  4. Press All, then Add. The effects appear in the MODE list straight away.

Not sure? Help > How to Use This App > Features > Add-on modes > ▶ Show me does it with you, using the free pack.

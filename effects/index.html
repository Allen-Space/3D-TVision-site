Installing

  1. Download the 3D Effects .zip. There is no need to unpack it.
  2. In 3D-TVision, open the MODE list.
  3. Under the white line, press the folder button and pick the .zip.
  4. Press All, then Add. The effects appear in the MODE list straight away.

Every effect keeps its own variants and sliders, and works with Save, Load and Reset like the built-in ones. Taking one off the menu (the − button) never deletes it - you can add it back from the same list.
